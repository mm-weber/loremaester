# Ironhold Council

The Ironhold Council is the governing body of [[Ironhold]], composed of five clan elders who collectively rule the dwarven stronghold. The council convenes in the [[Hall of Ancestors]], a sacred chamber deep within the city where the stone likenesses of past leaders watch over their deliberations.

## Structure

The council is made up of one elder from each of the five great clans of Ironhold. The eldest among them serves as head of the council, a position currently held by [[Thrain Ironbeard]] of the [[Ironbeard Clan]].

Decisions are reached by majority vote, though matters of war or treaty require unanimous agreement. Each elder speaks with the full authority of their clan.

## Meeting Place

The [[Hall of Ancestors]] serves as the council's seat of power. Carved from living rock and lined with statues of every elder who has ever served, the hall is both a political chamber and a shrine to dwarven tradition.

## Role

The Ironhold Council oversees all matters of governance within [[Ironhold]], including:
- Trade agreements and control of the mithril veins
- Defense of the [[Iron Mountains]]
- Disputes between the clans
- Relations with outside powers across [[Eryndor]]
