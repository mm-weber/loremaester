# Ironhold

> **Note**: This content was generated without access to existing vault notes. It may conflict with established lore. Review and reconcile before merging into the vault.

## Overview

Ironhold is a fortified city built into and around a series of iron-rich ridgelines. Known for its metalwork, martial traditions, and unyielding architecture, the city has long served as a bulwark against threats from the surrounding wilds. Its skyline is dominated by dark iron spires and the ever-present glow of forge-fires reflecting off low clouds.

## Districts

### The Anvil Quarter
The industrial heart of Ironhold. Rows of smithies, foundries, and workshops line narrow streets permanently hazed with coal smoke. The rhythmic clanging of hammers on metal never fully stops here -- even at night, the late-shift smiths keep their fires burning. Master crafters compete for commissions from the city's ruling council, and apprentices sleep in lofts above their workshops.

- **Notable feature**: The Street of Sparks, where the density of forges is so high that embers drift through the air like fireflies at dusk.
- **Smell**: Coal, hot metal, and quenching oil.

### The Bastion
The elevated military and administrative district, built on the highest ridge. The Bastion contains the city's garrison, the council chambers, and the homes of Ironhold's ruling families. Its walls are thicker than those surrounding the rest of the city, forming a citadel-within-a-city. Access is controlled through three gated checkpoints.

- **Notable feature**: The Iron Throne Hall, where the city council convenes beneath a vaulted ceiling studded with iron stars.
- **Atmosphere**: Orderly, quiet, patrolled.

### Cindermarket
The main commercial district, sprawling across a broad terrace between the Anvil Quarter and the Bastion. Cindermarket is where traders from across the region come to buy Ironhold's famous metalwork and sell the goods the city cannot produce itself -- textiles, grain, spices, and lumber. The market operates on a six-day cycle, with the seventh day reserved for maintenance and religious observance.

- **Notable feature**: The Weighmaster's Tower, a stone column at the market's center where all official trade weights are calibrated.
- **Smell**: Roasting meat, foreign spices, leather.

### The Undershafts
A network of tunnels, mines, and underground chambers beneath the city. Originally excavated to extract iron ore, many played-out shafts have been repurposed as storage, housing for the city's poorest residents, and -- rumor has it -- meeting places for less legitimate enterprises. The Undershafts are poorly lit and loosely governed.

- **Notable feature**: The Deepwell, an underground cistern that provides fresh water to the lower city.
- **Atmosphere**: Damp, echoic, lantern-lit. Watch patrols are infrequent.

### Hearthside
The residential district where most of Ironhold's working population lives. Stone row-houses with iron-framed windows line streets that climb the lower slopes of the ridgeline. Community life centers around shared courting yards where neighbors cook, mend, and gossip. Hearthside is dense but not squalid -- Ironhold's civic pride extends to its working quarters.

- **Notable feature**: The Lantern Steps, a long stairway connecting Hearthside to Cindermarket, lined with iron lantern posts that are lit each evening by the Lamplighters' Guild.

## Notable Landmarks

### The Great Forge
A massive communal forge maintained by the city at public expense. Any citizen-smith may use it for large projects that exceed the capacity of private workshops. The Great Forge is also where ceremonial weapons and armor are made for the city guard. It is said the forge has not gone cold in over two hundred years.

### The Iron Gate
The main entrance to the city, a towering double gate made of solid iron plates riveted to a timber frame. The gate bears the city's sigil and is flanked by two watchtowers. It takes a team of eight to open or close it.

### Remembrance Wall
A long iron wall in the Bastion district inscribed with the names of every Ironhold soldier who has fallen in defense of the city. Families leave small iron tokens at the base of their loved ones' names. The wall is considered sacred ground.

### The Sootspire
A natural rock formation at the city's northern edge, blackened by centuries of forge smoke. Local tradition holds that the first settlers of Ironhold lit their first forge fire at the base of the Sootspire. It now serves as an informal gathering place and landmark for travelers approaching from the north.

## Daily Life

**Morning**: The city wakes to the sound of forge bellows and the smell of coal fires being stoked. Workers in the Anvil Quarter begin their shifts at dawn. In Hearthside, families eat simple breakfasts of porridge, dark bread, and iron-brewed tea (a local specialty made from herbs grown in rooftop gardens, steeped in iron kettles that give it a distinctive mineral taste).

**Midday**: Cindermarket reaches its peak activity. Street vendors sell skewered meats, roasted root vegetables, and hard cider. Off-duty soldiers from the Bastion mingle with traders and crafters. The Weighmaster announces commodity prices from the tower.

**Afternoon**: The second forge shift begins. Apprentices practice in the training yards. Merchants close deals and load wagons. In the Undershafts, miners return from the deeper tunnels and the underground taverns begin to fill.

**Evening**: The Lamplighters make their rounds along the Lantern Steps and major thoroughfares. Families gather in Hearthside's courting yards for communal meals. The taverns and alehouses of Cindermarket do their best business. Storytellers and bards perform in the common rooms.

**Night**: The city quiets but never fully sleeps. Night-shift smiths work by firelight in the Anvil Quarter. The watch patrols the walls and gates. In the Undershafts, the city's shadow economy comes alive.

## Economy

Ironhold's economy revolves around iron mining and metalworking. The city exports:
- Finished ironwork (tools, weapons, armor, construction hardware)
- Raw iron ingots
- Specialty alloys (closely guarded recipes)

The city imports:
- Grain and foodstuffs (limited arable land)
- Timber (the surrounding hills are largely deforested)
- Textiles and leather
- Luxury goods

## Culture and Customs

- **The Forging Day**: An annual festival where apprentice smiths present their masterwork to the guild masters. Successful candidates are elevated to journeyman status. The day includes competitions, feasting, and a torchlit procession through Cindermarket.
- **Iron tokens**: Small shaped pieces of iron carried as personal talismans. Different shapes carry different meanings -- a hammer for strength, a shield for protection, a ring for fidelity.
- **"Iron pays iron"**: A local saying meaning debts of honor must be repaid in kind. Ironhold's culture places heavy emphasis on reciprocity and keeping one's word.
